# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Marketing project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in our community.

We value collaboration, transparency, and constructive communication. All contributors, maintainers, users, and community members are expected to follow these principles.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members should:

* Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
* Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
* Provide constructive feedback.
* Focus on what is best for the project and community.
* Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
* Help create a friendly environment for new contributors.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Offensive comments related to personal characteristics.
* Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Any behavior that negatively impacts the safety and trust of the community.

---

## Communication Guidelines

When participating in discussions, issues, pull requests, or reviews:

* Explain your reasoning clearly.
* Avoid aggressive or disrespectful language.
* Critique ideas, not people.
* Assume good intentions.
* Be patient with new contributors.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Applying these standards fairly.
* Reviewing reports of unacceptable behavior.
* Protecting the interests of contributors and users.
* Maintaining a healthy and productive community.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through the appropriate project channels.

For security-related concerns, please follow the instructions in:

`SECURITY.md`

For general community issues, open a private discussion with project maintainers whenever possible.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate actions, including:

* Warning the person involved.
* Removing inappropriate content.
* Temporarily restricting participation.
* Permanently banning participation in serious cases.

All enforcement decisions will be made fairly and with consideration of the circumstances.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* Repository contributions
* Issues and pull requests
* Discussions
* Documentation
* Community communication channels
* Any official Marketing project activity

---

## Acknowledgement

By participating in the Marketing project, you agree to respect this Code of Conduct and help maintain a positive community.

Thank you for helping make Marketing a welcoming open-source project.
