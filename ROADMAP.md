# Roadmap

## Marketing Project Roadmap

This roadmap describes the planned development direction of the Marketing project.

The roadmap may change based on community feedback, technical requirements, and project priorities.

---

# Vision

Marketing aims to become a modern, open-source marketing platform that enables individuals, teams, agencies, and organizations to plan, automate, analyze, and improve their marketing activities.

Our long-term goal is to provide a flexible ecosystem that can be extended through integrations, plugins, and community contributions.

# Release Strategy

Marketing follows Semantic Versioning:

```
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

Example:

```
1.0.0
1.1.0
2.0.0
```

Major versions may include breaking changes.
